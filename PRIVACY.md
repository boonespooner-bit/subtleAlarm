# Privacy Policy — Subtle Alarm

_Last updated: 21 August 2026_

Subtle Alarm does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising,
no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

## Network access

Subtle Alarm does not request the `INTERNET` permission. The app is
technically incapable of making a network connection, so no data can leave
your device.

## What is stored on your device

Your alarm settings — time, repeat days, chosen tone, fade-in length,
volume, and whether vibration and the breathing light are enabled — are
saved in the app's private storage on your device. This information never
leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Permissions and why they are needed

- **Alarms & reminders (exact alarm)** — to ring at the exact time you set.
- **Notifications** — to show the alarm and open the wake screen over the
  lock screen.
- **Full-screen intent** — to display the wake screen when the alarm rings.
- **Foreground service** — to keep playing the alarm tone reliably while it
  is ringing.
- **Vibrate** — for the optional gentle vibration.
- **Wake lock** — to keep the device awake while the alarm is ringing.
- **Run at startup** — to restore your alarm after the phone reboots.
- **Camera flash (torch)** — only if you enable the optional breathing
  light. The app uses the flash LED only as a light; it never opens the
  camera and never captures photos or video.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
