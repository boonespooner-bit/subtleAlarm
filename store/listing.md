Soft bells and a sunrise glow. Wakes you, and lets your partner keep sleeping.

## Full description (4000 max)
Most alarms are built to startle. If you share a bed, that means two people wake up when only one meant to.

Subtle Alarm is built the other way around: a single soft bell, a slow warm glow, and a sound that arrives so gradually the room barely notices. Everything about it is designed to reach you and stop there.

THREE BELLS, SYNTHESIZED — NOT SAMPLED
Every tone is generated by the app itself, so there are no compressed audio files and no hard edges:
• Dawn — a low singing bowl, one slow strike every nine seconds
• Aurora — two distant hand-bells, a gentle fifth apart
• Breeze — a slow music-box arpeggio

A SOUND THAT ARRIVES SLOWLY
Choose a fade-in of 30 seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes. The bell begins near silence and swells so gradually that by the time it is loud enough to wake you, it has been in the room for minutes.

FIRST LIGHT, NOT A FLOODLIGHT
The wake screen brightens from deep night to warm sunrise in step with the sound. If you like, the camera flash breathes along with it — climbing from off to just 5% brightness over five seconds, then fading back down over five more. An ember, not a flash.

GENTLE VIBRATION
Optional, and deliberately faint: a low-amplitude heartbeat rather than a buzz.

STOP IT WITHOUT FUMBLING
Press the power button. Press either volume key. Tap the dismiss button, or anywhere on the screen. Whatever your hand finds first in the dark will work.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No internet permission — the app cannot connect to a network at all
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code
• Under 40 KB
• Free and open source

WHAT IT DOES
One alarm. Repeat days. Per-alarm volume. Auto-silences after 15 minutes. Restores itself after a reboot, and rings through Do Not Disturb like any alarm clock.

WHAT IT DOESN'T
No snooze. No multiple alarms. No puzzles, no shaking, no barcode scanning. It does one thing, quietly.

For anyone who wakes before their partner — early shifts, early flights, early runs — and would rather not take someone else's morning with them.

### Closed testing (testers see this)

Thanks for helping test Subtle Alarm.

Set an alarm a few minutes out and let it ring. The bell should start almost inaudible and swell slowly — it is meant to wake you without waking the person next to you.

Please try dismissing three ways: the power button, either volume key, and the on-screen button.

Also worth trying: all three tones, a five-minute fade, and the breathing light.

Tell me about anything that fails to ring, won't dismiss, or wakes your partner.

### Production (first public release)

First release.

Wake to soft synthesized bells that fade in over up to five minutes, a sunrise glow that brightens with the sound, and an optional gentle vibration or slow breathing light.

Dismiss with the power button, either volume key, the on-screen button, or a tap anywhere.

No ads, no accounts, no internet permission.
